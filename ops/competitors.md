### What we take from this

1. **Lead with this week's news that changes someone's money**, named for whoever it affects: "Your home loan EMI could go up this Wednesday", not "What is the repo rate".
2. **Ask a big personal question** ("How much do you need to retire?"). It is Sharan's best format, and it works faceless with numbers on screen.
3. **Rank things** ("Which scheme pays the most?"). Bars are save-able, and our engine already draws them.
4. **On red market weeks, test one relatable, light reel.** It has to stay within the rules: no named securities, no predictions.
5. **What we can't copy:** faces and trending audio. Our edge is clean numbers, sources and speed. Audio is the big gap, so adding a trending sound by hand to the best reel each week is worth testing.
