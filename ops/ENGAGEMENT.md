# Engagement: comments on bigger creators' posts

Goal: profile visits from people who already watch Indian personal-finance creators. A comment
that adds one real number to the creator's point makes people tap @fix_mymoney.

## Daily batch (part of the daily run)
1. In the built-in browser, signed in as **@fix_mymoney** (check the avatar/profile link first;
   never the owner's personal account), open the newest posts (last 24 h first) of 6–8 creators:
   rotate through @casarthakahuja, @financewithsharan, @ankurwarikoo, @pranjalkamra, @nehanagar,
   @ishmohitarora, @ca_rachanaranade, @simply.kaasu, @labourlawadvisor, @anushkarathod98,
   plus 2–3 mid-size (20K–300K) Indian finance creators. Skip posts we already commented on
   (ops/comments.csv) and posts about a named stock, IPO or crypto coin.
2. Draft up to 8 comments, one per post:
   - 1–2 sentences, under 220 characters, Hinglish or English to match the post.
   - Adds ONE concrete thing: a ₹ example, the after-tax number, a rule people miss. Every
     number checked against a page opened today.
   - No "follow me", no links, no self-promotion, no emojis spam, no buy/sell/hold of any
     security, no promised returns. Not a question to the creator only to farm replies.
3. Send the drafts to the owner (SendUserMessage + push): creator, post link, comment text.
   **Post only the ones he approves in that session.** No reply = post nothing; never carry an
   approval over to another day.
4. Post the approved ones 2–4 minutes apart (window 1300x900 so the desktop comment box shows;
   textbox "Add a comment…", then "Post"), confirm each appears, log it in ops/comments.csv.
5. Stop at once on any "action blocked", "try again later" or verification prompt: post
   nothing more that day and tell the owner. Max 10 comments a day. No likes, follows or DMs
   at scale; nothing designed to look automated or to evade detection.

## Judging it (weekly review)
Account profile views and follows on comment days vs non-comment days, and replies/likes on
our comments. If profile views don't move after 2 weeks, change the creators or the style.
